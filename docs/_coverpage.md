# Zappy

> A multiplayer network game where teams of autonomous agents compete to gather resources and level up through cooperative elevation rituals.

**A Tribute to Zaphod Beeblebrox** – Teams of autonomous "Trantorians" compete on a toroidal grid to gather resources and evolve. The first team to promote at least **6 players** to **level 8** wins the match.

- 🎮 **Multiplayer Network Game** - Real-time competition between teams
- 🤖 **Autonomous AI Agents** - Intelligent bots with strategic decision making  
- 🎨 **Beautiful 3D Visualization** - Real-time graphical client with SFML/raylib
- 🌐 **Custom Protocol** - TCP-based communication between server and clients
- 🏗️ **Modular Architecture** - Separate server, GUI, and AI components
